# Buyer and Customer Demo Script

Use the fictional browser demo or a dedicated staging tenant. Never present sample records as real traction.

1. **Problem (30 sec):** suppliers receive messy RFQs and lose margin/control between request, spreadsheet pricing and customer response.
2. **RFQ (90 sec):** open a fictional request, show pasted-text AI extraction, human review and catalog matching. State that AI never chooses price, tax or approval.
3. **Quote (3 min):** create catalog and manual lines, show single currency, authoritative costs, minimum price and live margin. Trigger manager approval.
4. **Document (90 sec):** open the customer-safe preview/PDF and point out revision, expiry, terms and the absence of internal economics.
5. **Customer loop (2 min):** send in staging, open the bearer link, view, ask a question and accept. Show activity without revision mutation.
6. **Control (90 sec):** rotate/revoke the link, create a revision and show the prior sent snapshot remains unchanged.
7. **SaaS operation (90 sec):** show organization switching, roles, plan usage, Stripe-hosted billing, export and operational docs.
8. **Close:** describe the focused RFQ-to-response product, integration configuration and verified limitations. Do not state customer/revenue metrics unless independently documented.
