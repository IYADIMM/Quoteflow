# Strategic Buyer Profile

Best-fit acquirers already serve B2B suppliers and can distribute QuoteFlow or embed its workflow:

- vertical SaaS providers for distributors, contractors, equipment/service suppliers or manufacturers;
- lightweight CRM/proposal vendors seeking RFQ ingestion and commercial approval depth;
- procurement/supplier-network products missing the seller-side quotation workspace;
- regional digital-transformation firms with repeatable quoting implementations;
- search funds or micro-SaaS operators able to run founder-led validation.

The buyer should have PostgreSQL/JavaScript operations capability, customer discovery access and willingness to complete staging/provider/security qualification. A buyer seeking proven MRR or a certified regulated platform is a poor fit because this asset does not claim either.

Strategic value comes from compressing build time for a coherent workflow and retaining a focused product boundary. Synergies are strongest where the buyer can provide distribution, vertical templates, implementation support or integrations without turning the product into a broad ERP.
